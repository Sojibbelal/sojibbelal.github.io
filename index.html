01 / 03

Email Segmentation & Automation at Scale

Segmentation Automation Digioh

Situation

Digioh's customers were sending emails to their entire contact list with no segmentation. Everyone received the same message — regardless of their behavior, source, or stage in the customer journey.

Problem

Unsegmented sending meant low engagement, high unsubscribe rates, and missed revenue. The right message wasn't reaching the right person at the right time, which eroded trust in email as a channel.

What I Did

  • Audited existing contact lists and identified segmentation criteria — behavior, lead source, engagement level, and lifecycle stage
  • Split contacts into targeted lists based on those criteria
  • Built automation workflows that triggered personalised emails to each segment at the right time
  • Managed automated platform migrations for users moving their email data — reducing manual effort and minimising errors during transition
Outcome: Customers moved from batch-and-blast sending to targeted, automated email flows. Engagement improved as the right audiences received relevant content. Migration automation dramatically reduced turnaround time and human error.

02 / 03

Form-Driven Personalisation Engine

Form Logic List Segmentation Email Sequences

Situation

Clients had forms collecting rich user data — preferences, intent signals, self-identified needs — but that data wasn't driving any follow-up logic. Every user got the same email regardless of what they submitted.

Problem

Unused form data meant missed personalisation opportunity. The gap between what users told the brand and what the brand sent back was causing irrelevance and drop-off.

What I Did

  • Connected form inputs to segmentation logic — users were automatically placed into different lists based on their specific responses
  • Built triggered email sequences that fired immediately based on each input path
  • Designed multi-step journeys tailored to each segment, so every user received a relevant, contextual experience from submission onward
Outcome: Every form submission triggered a personalised email sequence. Forms became a live segmentation and personalisation engine — not just a data collection tool.

03 / 03

Help Documentation System & Knowledge Base Setup

Documentation Customer Success Onboarding

Situation

Support teams and customers were relying heavily on direct communication for questions that had repeatable, documentable answers. There was no centralised knowledge base.

Problem

Without structured documentation, the same questions consumed support hours repeatedly. Customers couldn't self-serve, which slowed their time-to-value and increased ticket volume.

What I Did

  • Identified the most common and high-impact support topics through ticket analysis
  • Wrote clear, structured help articles covering platform features, workflows, and troubleshooting steps
  • Organised the knowledge base into logical categories that matched how users actually searched for help
Outcome: Customers could self-serve for common questions, reducing support ticket volume and freeing the team to focus on complex issues. Onboarding became faster with structured reference material available from day one.
