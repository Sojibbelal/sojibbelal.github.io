Email Segmentation & Automation at Scale

SegmentationAutomationMigrationDigioh

Situation

A client in Digioh was sending emails to their entire contact list with no segmentation — everyone received the same message regardless of behavior, source, or lifecycle stage.

Problem

Unsegmented sending meant low engagement, high unsubscribes, and missed revenue. The right message wasn't reaching the right person at the right time.

What I Did

  • Audited existing contact lists and identified segmentation criteria — behavior, lead source, engagement level, lifecycle stage
  • Split contacts into targeted lists and built automation workflows for each segment
  • Handled automated platform migrations, reducing manual effort and errors
Outcome: Customers moved from batch-and-blast to targeted, automated flows. Engagement improved and migration turnaround time dropped significantly.

Form LogicSegmentationEmail Sequences

03 / 07

HubSpot CRM Integration & Contact Sync

HubSpotCRM IntegrationRevOps

Situation

A client ran HubSpot alongside a separate CRM, with contacts living in two disconnected systems. Sales and marketing were working from different, often conflicting data.

Problem

Duplicate records, stale contact data, and missed handoffs between teams. There was no single source of truth for the customer record.

What I Did

  • Mapped the data schema between HubSpot and the existing CRM to identify field alignment and conflicts
  • Built a two-way sync keeping contacts, lifecycle stages, and properties current in both systems
  • Set up automated segmentation so newly synced contacts dropped into the right lists immediately
  • Created workflows triggering onboarding or nurture sequences based on CRM contact status
Outcome: Sales and marketing operated from a single live contact database. Leads no longer fell through the gap and automated follow-ups fired without manual intervention.

MailchimpFormsList ManagementCampaigns
